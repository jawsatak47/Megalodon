Meet Jaws

Anthony "Jaws" Vella

Twenty-plus years in, this still started the same way most of it did: a wall, a can, and no permission. Graffiti came first. Everything else grew out from there.

Since then it's stretched into fine art, hand lettering, sign painting and gold leaf — trained the traditional way, under working sign painters — plus murals, illustration, design, and a hip-hop project that runs parallel to all of it. QuarkForce is where the characters and the science-fiction side of the brain live. JawsAtak47 is where the music does.

None of it is a side hustle to the other. It's one practice wearing different tools.
